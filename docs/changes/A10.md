# A10: signed merchant quotes and durable ticket delivery

## Summary

Add real EIP-712 quotes from configured merchant identities, with catalog-only
prices and onchain vault/allowlist validation. A durable watcher queues Locked
events, prepares one immutable ticket bundle, anchors its hash through fulfill,
and publishes the ticket only after the chain confirms delivery.

## Files

- apps/merchants/src: quote service, SQLite store, Arc adapter, watcher and routes
- apps/merchants/package.json and .env.example
- packages/shared/src/authorization.ts: quote terms may be hashed before signing

## Interfaces touched

POST /quotes; catalog includes each configured merchant's actual address.
GET /tickets/:token renders the paid ticket and QR. Server-authenticated
GET /internal/tickets/:vault/:proposalId returns the immutable bundle and hash.
Add MERCHANT_DATABASE_PATH, MERCHANTS_API_SECRET, MERCHANT_POLL_INTERVAL_MS and
ARC_START_BLOCK. The viem dependency signs/submits transactions; qrcode and its
types provide the required ticket QR without a remote rendering service.

## Verification

Tests validate real quote signatures, reject caller-selected prices/payees,
recover a finalized payment after receipt loss, replay watcher cursors without
duplicate payment, render a ticket QR and enforce internal ticket authentication.
Existing health/catalog tests use an explicitly injected in-memory database.
