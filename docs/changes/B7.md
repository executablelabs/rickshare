# B7: the Plan feed

## Summary

Plan is now a live conversation with the planner. Members ask for ideas, and the planner's replies appear with each proposal's ticket underneath. The trip's server-sent events keep messages, tickets and balances current without reloading. On a ticket waiting for them, a member can tap Approve my share: World checks it's really them for that exact share, then their own wallet sends the vault's approve and their stamp presses onto the ticket. Or they can tap Not for me, which sends the prepared reject. Members outside a purchase see it collapsed with nothing to approve. Fulfilled tickets offer View my ticket.

## Files

- apps/web/src/lib/plan.ts: chat and proposals queries, message merging, stream events, the approve call, tickets
- apps/web/src/components/plan/{Feed,Composer,ShareActions}.tsx: the feed, message box and share actions
- apps/web/src/components/plan/ProposalTicket.tsx: the stamp press on the member who just approved
- apps/web/src/routes/trips.$tripId.plan.tsx: optimistic sending, planning indicator, stream
- apps/web/src/styles/tokens.css: `stamp-press` (scale 1.12 to 1, rotate -6deg to -2deg, 180ms, none under reduced motion)
- apps/web/src/components/shell/AppShell.tsx: the money panel stays in view while the feed scrolls
- apps/web/src/test/setup.ts: findBy queries wait up to 5s under the full suite
- tests: message merging and dedupe, stream events, approve encoding, empty state and sending, failed send, decline, World-then-wallet approval, collapsed not-for-you ticket

None. This uses the existing B6 chat and stream, A8 approval, A9 proposal and ticket, and B8 action routes.
