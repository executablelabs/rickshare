# B7: the Plan feed

## Summary

Plan is now a live conversation with the planner. Members ask for ideas, and the planner's replies appear with each proposal's ticket underneath. The trip's server-sent events keep messages, tickets and balances current without reloading. On a ticket waiting for them, a member can tap Approve my share: World checks it's really them for that exact share, then their own wallet sends the vault's approve and their stamp presses onto the ticket. Or they can tap Not for me, which sends the prepared reject. Members outside a purchase see it collapsed with nothing to approve. Fulfilled tickets offer View my ticket.

Review fixes bind approvals to the displayed member, vault, proposal, amount and fresh deadline before wallet submission. Interrupted chat retries retain their request id, stopped requests restore the draft, and streamed replies complete requests even if their HTTP response fails. Malformed stream frames refresh the feed, expired proposals remove approval actions, ticket links open from the click, and unbroken chat text wraps on phones. Integrating the reviewed onboarding preserves ENS names, transaction receipt recovery and the development-only inert preview.
